## KEY CONTRASTS

**say_more vs press_for_reasoning**
- "Was there a cover?" → say_more (detail)
- "Why do you think that?" → press (reasoning)
- "What do you mean?" → say_more (clarification)
- "What's your evidence?" → press (evidence)
- "Because what?" → say_more (complete thought)

**say_more (Type A) vs add_on (Type B)**
- Same student continues → say_more
- Different student responds → add_on
- "Tell me more" → say_more
- "Anyone else?" → add_on
