## CRITICAL CONTRAST PAIRS (study these to avoid common confusions)

### CONTRAST 1: say_more vs press_for_reasoning

| Turn | Code | Key reason |
|------|------|-----------|
| S: "It was on the rooftop." T: "Was there a cover?" | say_more | Detail question, no reasoning demand |
| S: "The rooftop is the best spot." T: "Why do you think it's the best?" | press | Evaluative CLAIM + explicit WHY |
| S: "I like the blue one." T: "Tell me more about that." | say_more | Elaboration request |
| S: "I like the blue one." T: "Why blue and not red?" | press | Demands justification for preference |
| S: "They moved to the city." T: "What happened after?" | say_more | Narrative continuation |
| S: "They moved to the city." T: "Why did they move?" | press | Causal explanation demand |
| S: "I think it's unfair." T: "What do you mean unfair?" | say_more | Clarification |
| S: "I think it's unfair." T: "What evidence shows it's unfair?" | press | Evidence demand for claim |

### CONTRAST 2: say_more (Type A) vs add_on (Type B)

| Context | Code | Key reason |
|---------|------|-----------|
| S_A says X. T: "Tell me more." S_A continues. | say_more | Same student continues |
| S_A says X. T: "Anyone else?" S_B responds. | add_on | Different student responds |
| S_A says X. T: "What about you, Maria?" Maria responds. | add_on | Teacher names different student |
| S_A says X. T: "Can you give an example?" S_A responds. | say_more | Same student, elaboration |

### CONTRAST 3: press_for_reasoning (Type A) vs add_on (Type B)

| Context | Code | Key reason |
|---------|------|-----------|
| S_A: "It's unfair." T: "Why do you say that?" S_A responds. | press | Same student, reasoning demand |
| S_A: "It's unfair." T: "Who else has thoughts on this?" S_B responds. | add_on | Different student, new ideas |
| S_A: "It's unfair." T: "Does anyone have evidence for or against?" S_B responds. | add_on | Different student invited |
