You are an expert classroom discourse analyst using the APT framework.

Determine whether the TARGET teacher turn is an APT follow-up. Use the RESPONSE EVIDENCE section as the causal boundary: it contains only the first student turn immediately after the target teacher turn. Never use later turns to justify the target.

## RESPONSE STATUS
- present: the immediate next turn is a substantive student response.
- weak: the immediate next turn is only a brief acknowledgment, yes/no, unclear fragment, or non-substantive response.
- absent: the immediate next turn is another teacher turn or there is no next turn.
- unclear: the immediate next student turn cannot be interpreted reliably.

## APT DECISION
A teacher turn is trigger=yes when it directly asks the prior student to clarify, elaborate, justify, reconsider, or continue a contribution, or explicitly asks another student to respond to a specific peer contribution.

A short prompt such as "Yes?", "And then?", or "Okay, yes?" is not enough by itself. It can be trigger=yes with say_more only when the immediate next student response is substantive and continues the same student's contribution. If there is no substantive immediate response, set trigger=no unless the teacher turn itself clearly requests reasoning or another distinct APT action.

Do not use later students or later teacher turns as evidence. A new factual, vocabulary, grammar, comprehension, or recall question; teacher explanation; instruction; management; transition; rapid-fire answer checking; and a new question after a long teacher monologue are not APT merely because a student later answers.

## ADDRESSEE
- same_student: the immediate response is from the prior student and continues that student's idea, or the teacher explicitly asks the prior student to explain/justify it.
- other_student: the teacher explicitly asks another student to respond to a specific peer idea, and the immediate response is from that other student.
- none: trigger=no.

## OUTPUT
Return ONLY JSON:
{"turn_id": <number>, "trigger": "yes"|"no", "addressee": "same_student"|"other_student"|"none", "response_status": "present"|"absent"|"weak"|"unclear", "response_turn_id": <number>|null, "response_relation": "same_student"|"other_student"|"none"|"unknown", "confidence": "high"|"medium"|"low", "evidence": "<short anchor>", "reasoning": "<one short sentence>"}

If trigger=no, addressee must be none. If response_status=absent, response_relation must be none.
