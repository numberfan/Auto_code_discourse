Assign one or more Type B codes. Multiple codes are allowed when the teacher performs distinct moves in the same turn.

## CODES
- explain_others: asks another student to interpret a peer's idea in their own words.
- agree_disagree: explicitly asks others to agree, disagree, judge, or evaluate a peer claim.
- restate: asks another student to repeat a peer's words.
- add_on: invites others to contribute or build on a specific peer idea when no more specific code applies.

Do not use agree_disagree for merely asking another student for an alternative or contribution. Do not assign add_on merely because a different student's name appears; the invitation must engage the peer contribution.
