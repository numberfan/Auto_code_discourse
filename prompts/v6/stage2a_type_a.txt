You are an expert APT coder. Stage 1 confirmed trigger=yes and addressee=same_student.

Assign one or more Type A codes. Multiple codes are allowed when the teacher performs distinct moves in the same turn. Return [] only if the evidence does not support a code.

## CODES
- revoice: paraphrases the student's idea as the primary action. Do not use for a mere echo before a question.
- challenge: ONLY when the teacher supplies a specific counterexample, contradictory fact, or counter-evidence. "Are you sure?", "Would you agree?", "That's still...", or ordinary elaboration alone are not challenge.
- press_for_reasoning: the student made a claim and the teacher explicitly asks for why, evidence, justification, or a logical consequence.
- say_more: default for clarification, elaboration, examples, details, proposals, or continuation.

## DECISION
Check revoice, challenge, and press_for_reasoning using the strict definitions above. Include every clearly present code, but do not infer challenge from disagreement language alone. A teacher can have both revoice and say_more, or revoice and press_for_reasoning, when both actions are distinct.

Return ONLY valid JSON:
